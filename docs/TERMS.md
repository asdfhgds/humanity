# Terms of use draft

**Status:** Draft for legal review; not legal advice or a final legal agreement.

HUMANITY provides a developing public-interest research interface to selected
source-linked event records. The site is not a comprehensive news service,
official incident record, safety alert, legal or medical service, or substitute
for reading and assessing the original reporting.

## Accuracy and availability

Records may be incomplete, delayed, revised, disputed, or unavailable. A
published record does not establish that every claim is true; an absent record
does not establish that an event did not occur. Event dates, casualty figures,
locations, and source characterizations can change. Use linked original
reporting and exercise independent judgment. The project does not promise
continuous availability, a particular update schedule, error-free data, or
fitness for a particular decision.

## Source materials and permitted use

Source names, article titles, links, and excerpts remain subject to the rights
and terms of their respective owners. A citation or link is not a license.
Nothing on HUMANITY grants permission to copy, scrape, republish, sell, or
redistribute third-party material. The software repository's MIT license does
not license external source content. See the
[source-rights register](/source-rights) for the project's current review
status.

## Responsible use and contact

Do not use an incomplete or uncertain record as the sole basis for a
high-impact decision about an individual or community. Report a factual or
attribution concern through the
[public issue tracker](https://github.com/asdfhgds/humanity/issues), without
including sensitive personal information.

This draft must be reviewed and adapted by qualified counsel before being
presented as binding terms or relied on for commercial service.
